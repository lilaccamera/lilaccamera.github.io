Lilac Camera

Sony's camera app for the Xperia XZ1 Compact, rebuilt for custom ROMs, with GCam multi-frame processing

Get started For ROM maintainers

Lilac is a camera app for one phone. It rebuilds Sony's camera app, with all of the camera's hardware features, for custom ROMs, and adds GCam's multi-frame processing on top.

Fully works on sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2 only

Sony's camera features¶

Sony's camera app uses the phone's hardware fully but does not run on custom ROMs. Lilac brings its features back: face frames, subject tracking, the laser distance sensor, the two-stage camera key, smile shutter, Sony's own photo processing, Sony's video stabilisers, and 960 fps slow motion with 60 and 120 fps video. The aim is the whole of Sony's camera app; the Sony camera features page lists what is done and what is not yet.

No code from Sony's camera app is in Lilac. Its Sony features were rebuilt by studying how Sony's app talks to the phone's camera system, which settings it sends and which system services it calls, and making the same requests from Lilac's own code. Where a feature needs Sony's own services, such as cacao for slow motion, Lilac calls the ones already on the phone's ROM; it does not ship them.

The XZ1 Compact's sensor records at 960 frames a second, but on custom ROMs that went away with Sony's camera app. sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2 keeps Sony's slow-motion service, cacao, and Lilac reaches it through its companion app, the Lilac Sony Bridge. So 960 fps works again.

GCam's multi-frame processing¶

GCam processes photos well but knows nothing about Sony's camera. Lilac runs GCam's HDR+ processing on Sony's camera hardware, next to the Sony features above.

What comes from GCam

PhotonCamera rebuilt GCam's HDR+ pipeline from the GCam ports MGC and AGC, to run through Android's standard camera interface on the graphics chip. Lilac's Pro mode carries it with GCam's own model files:

GCam In Lilac
HDR+: a burst of darker RAW frames Pro's HDR+ frames and the darker burst
Sabre, the handheld merge the Sabre merge mode (default)
HDR+ spatial merge with brackets the Spatial merge mode
Learned auto exposure the same models, capped by the HDR ratio (8× by default; GCam's default is 9.8×)
HDRNet tone mapping the same HDRNet model
Denoise tuning GCam's luma, chroma and Sabre settings

What Lilac adds: Pro photos are developed one at a time in a separate process that waits for free memory, so they fit in 4 GB; Photo, Night and Portrait stack without RAW, on every ROM; and Sony's camera features that GCam does not know.

Get Lilac¶

Lilac fully works on sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2 only. Other lilac ROMs get basic multi-frame stacking, because they lack the raw19 camera shim and Sony's cacao service.

Feature Needs on the ROM Tested on
Photo, Night, Portrait, Video: multi-frame stacking any lilac ROM, Android 11 or newer
Sony's faces, subject tracking, laser focus and camera key Sony's camera driver; expected on any lilac ROM sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2 only
Pro (RAW): GCam's HDR+ and HDRNet the raw19 camera shim sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2
960 fps slow motion, 60 and 120 fps video, Sony's stabilisers Sony's cacao service and the Lilac Sony Bridge sunplex07's LineageOS 23.2 only

Other phones are not tested. Testers can add the bridge themselves: install the bridge. ROM maintainers: ROM integration.

  • Stable: LilacCamera.apk from the latest release. Lilac ROMs ship this same APK, so a ROM's copy also updates from new releases.
  • Beta: LilacBeta.apk from a pre-release. It installs beside the stable copy as Lilac Beta.

Rebuilt for a 4.6-inch screen¶

Lilac's interface is not PhotonCamera's. The camera, the gallery and the editor were rebuilt around one rule: a 4.6-inch phone held in one hand.

  1. Everything you press is in thumb reach. Holding the phone in one hand, your thumb reaches the lower part of the screen, shaded in the animation. Every button sits there; the top of the screen is only picture.
  2. The picture takes the gestures: swipe down for everyday settings, up for manual controls, sideways for looks.
  3. The shutter is the mode dial, with the zoom control just above it, in reach of the thumb.
  4. The side keys are camera controls: half-press the camera key to focus, press fully to shoot; the volume keys shoot or zoom.
