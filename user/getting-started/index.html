Getting started¶

First launch¶

Lilac asks for the camera and the microphone. On Android 13 and newer it also asks for notifications. They are optional: they only show the progress of Pro photos that finish in the background.

There is no tour. A small pill, How to use Lilac, sits above the shutter in Photo mode until you open it once. It walks through modes, quick settings, manual controls, zoom and looks. To see it again, open quick settings and tap the info mark next to the mode name.

The camera screen¶

  • Top line: small marks for anything switched on or not at its default, such as a timer (3s), Live, Smile, flash or a light reading like 12 lux. Tap them to open the everyday row. Sony's scene icons can appear at the right end.
  • Above the shutter: quick settings on the left, the zoom control (a thumbwheel) in the middle, looks on the right.
  • Bottom row: the latest photo on the left, the shutter with the mode dial in the middle, the camera switch on the right.

Tap the picture to focus and meter there. A long press locks focus. Pinch to zoom.

Modes¶

Swipe sideways on the shutter to change mode, or tap the faint mode mark beside it. From left to right:

Mode Mark For
Night dim scenes; stacks every shot
Portrait people, with the background blurred
Pro M RAW photos, developed by Lilac
Photo AUTO everyday shots
Video video

A short flick moves one mode; a slow swipe can cross them all. The dial stops at Night and at Video. It is locked while a video records.

Pro is only on the dial when the camera gives apps RAW frames. On the front camera, or on a ROM without the raw19 shim, the dial has no M.

Quick settings¶

Tap quick settings . A sheet rises from the bottom; tap the picture or drag the sheet down to close it. Group titles are off by default, to keep the lists short; switch them on with Group titles in Settings.

  • The everyday row on top: Live, timer, Smile, aspect and flash in the photo modes; resolution, frame rate, torch and stabilisation in Video. Tap an item to step to its next value; its value shows inside or beside the icon. You can also swipe down on the picture to show just this row.
  • Below it are two tabs. Shooting holds what changes the shot (Lens, Stacking, Exposure, HDR+ and the mode's own settings), Output what changes the file. Swipe sideways or tap a tab. The same row has the Toolbox, Content management and Settings icons. The front camera has one page and no tabs.
  • Tap a setting's name for a one-line explanation. Long-press it to open the full setting in Settings.

Symbols on the camera screen¶

Manual controls¶

How to use the manual controls

Swipe up on the picture. The values appear along its bottom edge: EV (exposure), Tv (shutter), ISO, AF (focus) and AWB (white balance). Portrait adds Av (blur strength); Night adds Frames.

  • A value on Auto shows the camera's live reading, such as 1/60 or ISO 400.
  • Drag a value sideways to change it. A ruler shows while you drag.
  • Tap a value to keep its ruler open. Tap A on the ruler, or double-tap the value, to go back to Auto.
  • A value you set by hand gets a small amber dot and stays on screen.
  • Focus has an H button for hyperfocal focus.
  • EV only works while exposure is on Auto.
  • Pro opens with the values showing. In other modes they hide about 5 s after your last touch.
  • The front camera has no manual shutter or ISO. Tapping them says "Not available on this camera".

Zoom¶

Turn the thumbwheel above the shutter with your thumb: its rim follows your finger, holds for a moment at each stop with a tick, coasts after a flick, and a tap on a number goes there. While you zoom the note line shows the zoom and its 35 mm focal length. Pinching reaches the full range.

In Settings › Setup › Interface, Zoom control can be changed to a simple pill (the same, with a white disc) or the collar: a curved band that zooms faster the harder you pull it sideways, left to zoom in, and springs back. Tap T or W on the collar to step, or hold them to keep zooming. With Volume Key Action set to Focal Switch, the volume buttons step the zoom too (see Buttons on the side).

Buttons on the side¶

The XZ1 Compact's side buttons work as camera controls, so you can shoot without reaching across the screen:

  • Camera key, half-press: focuses and holds focus and exposure while you keep it there. See Focus and faces.
  • Camera key, full press: takes the photo, or starts and stops a video. During a slow-motion recording it fires the 960 fps burst.
  • Volume buttons: take the photo by default. In Settings › Setup › Controls › Volume Key Action you can make them Focal Switch (zoom: up steps in, down steps out, one stop at a time, like tapping T or W), Exposure Comp. (up brightens, down darkens) or Volume Control (they change the volume as usual).

Looks¶

A look is a colour style (a LUT). Swipe sideways on the picture to step through your favourite looks. Tap for all of them, with Intensity, colour recipe, frame and presets. A Manage tile at the end of the strip opens content management.

In Night that button becomes Night's Fast or Accurate switch instead. Looks cannot change while a video records.

The latest photo¶

Tap the thumbnail to open the viewer on the photo still processing, or else the newest photo or video. Its edge shows processing: a running arc while a photo is processed, a dotted ring while photos wait, pause bars when processing is paused, and an amber ring when Android keeps stopping a Pro photo for memory. See Pro mode.
